\section*{Lab summary for the instructor}

\textbf{Learning objective:} The engineer can inject a GPS glitch via SITL parameters, observe the EKF3 lane-switch STATUSTEXT, and locate the corresponding log event in the dataflash. Secondary: trace the switch from the observed STATUSTEXT back to \texttt{NavEKF3::switchLane} (\citeline{libraries/AP\_NavEKF3/AP\_NavEKF3.cpp}{1076}) and \texttt{NavEKF3\_core::errorScore} (\citerange{libraries/AP\_NavEKF3/AP\_NavEKF3\_Outputs.cpp}{62}{86}).

\textbf{Depth:} internals --- the engineer should describe, at the level of function names and line numbers, why lane 1 was chosen over lane 0.

\textbf{Feed-forward:} The EKF lane-switch arbitration is the primary feed-forward from M7 to the capstone (L5 Eng 3). An engineer who sees the switch live here will reason correctly about \texttt{errorScore()} in the capstone extraction.

\section*{Pointers to advanced material}

\instadvanced{When an engineer asks ``how does \texttt{errorScore} decide to switch?'': M7 derives the complete formula. Score is a weighted MAX of \texttt{velTestRatio}, \texttt{posTestRatio}, \texttt{hgtTestRatio}, with airspeed and magnetometer gates controlled by \texttt{EKF\_AFFINITY\_ARSP} and \texttt{EKF\_AFFINITY\_MAG}. The lane switch fires when the backup's score is consistently lower than the primary's by \texttt{EK3\_ERR\_THRESH} (default 0.2, line 638 of \codepath{AP\_NavEKF3.cpp}). Reference Eng 3's capstone for the in-depth treatment.}

\section*{What you will do}

In this lab you will fly a simulated ArduPlane in \flmode{FBWA} mode and deliberately break its GPS sensor while it is airborne. You will watch the EKF3 sensor-fusion engine detect the bad data and switch to its backup estimation lane.

The key output is a GCS STATUSTEXT line reading \texttt{EKF3 lane switch 1} --- a message emitted by \texttt{NavEKF3::switchLane} at \citeline{libraries/AP\_NavEKF3/AP\_NavEKF3.cpp}{1076}. After the lane switch you will restore the GPS, land, download the dataflash log, and confirm the event is recorded.

This lab makes the EKF lane-health arbitration described in Module M7 tangible: you can see \texttt{errorScore()} acting on real (simulated) sensor data rather than reading about it in isolation.

\section*{The steps}

\subsection*{Step 1 --- Start SITL at KSFO with the lab params}
In Terminal A:
\begin{Verbatim}[frame=single,fontsize=\small]
Tools/autotest/sim_vehicle.py -v ArduPlane -f plane --console --map -L KSFO --no-rebuild
\end{Verbatim}
Wait for \texttt{APM: EKF3 IMU0 origin set} in the console (or until the map shows a blue plane icon at San Francisco).

In MAVProxy:
\begin{Verbatim}[frame=single,fontsize=\small]
param load course/labs/gnc-plane-3day-pilot-l3-gps-ekf-laneswitch/params.parm
\end{Verbatim}

\subsection*{Step 2 --- Confirm dual EKF lanes}
\begin{Verbatim}[frame=single,fontsize=\small]
param show EK3_IMU_MASK
\end{Verbatim}
Expected: \texttt{EK3\_IMU\_MASK 3.000000}.

\subsection*{Step 3 --- Take off and cruise}
\begin{Verbatim}[frame=single,fontsize=\small]
mode TAKEOFF
arm throttle
\end{Verbatim}
When altitude $> 50$ m:
\begin{Verbatim}[frame=single,fontsize=\small]
mode FBWA
\end{Verbatim}
Fly steady $\sim$10 s.

\subsection*{Step 4 --- Inject GPS noise}
\begin{Verbatim}[frame=single,fontsize=\small]
param set SIM_GPS1_NOISE 5
\end{Verbatim}
The plane keeps flying. The EKF sees noisier GPS measurements.

\subsection*{Step 5 --- Inject a GPS glitch}
\begin{Verbatim}[frame=single,fontsize=\small]
param set SIM_GPS1_GLTCH_X 50
\end{Verbatim}
Within 30 s, the console should print:
\begin{Verbatim}[frame=single,fontsize=\small]
EKF3 lane switch 1
\end{Verbatim}
The EKF detected the bad lane and switched. Message comes from \texttt{NavEKF3::switchLane} after \texttt{checkLaneSwitch} computes that the backup lane's score is lower than the primary.

\subsection*{Step 6 --- Restore the GPS}
\begin{Verbatim}[frame=single,fontsize=\small]
param set SIM_GPS1_GLTCH_X 0
param set SIM_GPS1_NOISE 0
\end{Verbatim}
Plane should continue normally. If you see \texttt{failsafe} in the console, raise your hand.

\subsection*{Step 7 --- Return to land}
\begin{Verbatim}[frame=single,fontsize=\small]
mode RTL
\end{Verbatim}
Wait for the plane to return.

\subsection*{Step 8 --- Download and inspect the log}
\begin{Verbatim}[frame=single,fontsize=\small]
disarm
\end{Verbatim}
In Terminal B:
\begin{Verbatim}[frame=single,fontsize=\small]
python3 Tools/autotest/mavlogdump.py --types=EV logs/00000001.BIN
\end{Verbatim}
Look for an \texttt{EV} record around the time of the lane switch.

\section*{What success looks like}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Console shows \texttt{EKF3 lane switch 1} (or another digit) within 30 s of the glitch.
  \item \texttt{mavlogdump.py --types=EV} prints at least one line.
  \item Plane does not enter failsafe after GPS is restored.
\end{itemize}

If \texttt{EKF3 lane switch} never appears, the glitch may not exceed the EKF lane-switch threshold, or \texttt{EK3\_IMU\_MASK} is not 3. Try \texttt{param set SIM\_GPS1\_GLTCH\_X 100}.

\section*{Common mistakes and quick fixes}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{\texttt{EKF3 lane switch} never appears} --- check \texttt{EK3\_IMU\_MASK} is 3. If 1, only one lane runs and there is nothing to switch to.
  \item \textbf{\texttt{SIM\_GPS1\_GLTCH\_X} parameter not found} --- binary built before GPS subgroup prefix rename. Confirm SITL binary matches repo: \texttt{./waf plane --no-configure}.
  \item \textbf{Plane enters failsafe immediately after \texttt{SIM\_GPS1\_GLTCH\_X 50}} --- glitch too large. Switch to \flmode{RTL}, restore, retry with smaller glitch (e.g. 30).
  \item \textbf{No EV records in log} --- \texttt{LOG\_BITMASK} may not be 65535. \texttt{param show LOG\_BITMASK}; set if needed.
  \item \textbf{\texttt{mavlogdump.py} says ``file not found''} --- log number may differ. \texttt{ls logs/*.BIN} to find the actual filename.
\end{enumerate}
